# Product

## Idea

Thapar Talks is a private campus social application inspired by familiar photo-sharing apps. Students can quickly see what is happening on campus, share posts, view stories, discover clubs, and connect with other students.

The product should feel easy and visual. It does not need a large platform architecture for the first usable version.

## Audience

- TIET students
- Student clubs and societies
- Campus event organizers

## Current demo

The frontend demo includes:

- Responsive home feed
- Campus stories
- Likes and saves
- Follow interactions
- Suggested people and clubs
- Post creation modal
- Mobile and desktop navigation
- Local demo media that works without internet access
- Working post creation, comments, likes, saves, follows, and story navigation
- Mock Google/Thapar sign-in and account onboarding
- Search, Explore, Reels, direct messages and notifications
- Profile grids, saved content, tagged content, settings and logout
- Full post detail, comments and content-management flows
- Story creation and highlight management
- Reel creation, editing and video playback
- Other-user profiles, follower lists and group messaging
- Password recovery and account deletion interfaces

All content currently comes from mock data.

## MVP scope

The first connected version should add only:

1. Student sign-in
2. User profiles
3. Create and view posts
4. Likes and comments
5. Follow and unfollow
6. Basic communities or clubs
7. Image upload

## Not required for the MVP

- Reels recommendation engine
- Livestreaming
- Payments or advertisements
- Automated AI moderation
- Complex admin and analytics systems

Additional infrastructure can be reconsidered only after the basic product works and there is a demonstrated need.
